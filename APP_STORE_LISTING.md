## Version 1.0 (App Store → iOS App → 1.0)
Promotional text (≤170):
Six taps a day. See what your meds are actually doing.

Description:
Dose is a 30-second daily check-in for people on psychiatric medication who want to know whether a change is helping.

Every day you answer six quick questions on a 1–5 scale — Mood, Depression, Focus, Overwhelm, Sleep, and whether you were active — tick off the meds you took, and add a note if you want. That's it.

Then Dose does the part your memory can't:

• WEEKLY AVERAGES at the top of every day, with arrows against last week
• PREVIOUS DAYS — one compact card per day: date, all six answers, and a day score
• TRENDS — 2-week, 30-day, and 90-day charts for every question
• MED CHANGE MARKERS — every dose change is drawn on the charts, so you can see what happened after
• BEFORE / AFTER — for each change, your averages in the two weeks before vs. the two weeks after, colored by whether they moved the right way
• MED LOG — your current meds and a history of every start, increase, decrease, and stop

Bring the charts to your prescriber. Stop guessing.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There is no account and no server. Everything stays on your iPhone. Export your data as a file whenever you like; delete the app and it's gone.

NOT MEDICAL ADVICE
Dose is a tracking tool, not a doctor. Medication decisions belong with your prescriber.
