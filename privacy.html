DDose

Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

Short version: Dose does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Dose stores

Dose is a daily check-in for tracking how you feel alongside your medications. It stores, on your device only:

  • Your daily answers (mood, depression, focus, overwhelm, sleep, activity) and optional notes
  • The list of medications you enter, and which ones you marked as taken each day
  • Your medication change log (started / increased / decreased / stopped)

Where it goes

Nowhere. Dose has no account system, no server, and no analytics. Data is saved in the app's local storage on your iPhone. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else.

Export and deletion

You can export your data as a JSON file at any time from the Meds tab, and import it on another device. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Health information

The information you enter may include personal health details. Because it never leaves your device, Dose does not process, store, or share health information on any server. Dose does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Children

Dose is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Dose is a personal tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice. Decisions about your medication should be made with your prescriber.
